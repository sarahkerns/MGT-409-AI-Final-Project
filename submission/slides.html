The Front Porch Exchange

Where the generations meet again.

A short chat with an AI guide turns "I could teach that" into a free 90-minute porch gathering, with neighbors already matched and invited.

Sarah Kerns · October 2026 · github.com/sarahkerns/MGT-409-AI-Final-Project

The problem

Who has itRural neighbors, as in Greenville, Texas: adults with skills nobody asks them to share (mending, baking, changing a tire), and people who want to learn or just want company.
How they copeWord of mouth, church or library groups, flyers, Facebook or Nextdoor posts, drop-in meetups. The host does all the planning and recruiting.
What it costsHours of planning, and the confidence to use apps. When hosting feels like too much work, it doesn't happen, and people stay home.
Why it mattersKnowing as few as six neighbors reduces the likelihood of loneliness.
Source: Nextdoor

How the agents work

Three gpt-6-luna agents hand off JSON, not the chat. Python tools do the parts that must be exact. Demo neighbors are fictional sample data.
